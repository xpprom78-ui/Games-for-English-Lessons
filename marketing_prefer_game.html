How to play

  • Tap Spin — it randomly picks ONE of 6 structures: prefer+noun, prefer+gerund, prefer+infinitive, would prefer+infinitive, would prefer+gerund (разговорно), or would rather+base verb.
  • A marketing dilemma (two options) appears.
  • Answer out loud using exactly the structure shown — not the one you'd naturally pick.
  • Tap Show model answer to check yourself, then Next dilemma.
